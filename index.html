Select Embedding Type

Choose how sentence coordinates are determined before dimensionality reduction:

Option A ✓

Spelling Embedding

Sentences that contain similar words or have similar spelling patterns will be positioned closer together.

Character N-Grams: "machine" and "machinery" will land close together; "dogs" and "puppies" land far apart.
Option B ✓

Meaning Embedding (Semantic)

Sentences with similar meanings or semantic concepts will be positioned closer together, even if they use different words.

Semantic Space: "I love dogs." and "I adore puppies." land close together; "The stock market is falling." lands far apart.

📚 Educational Takeaway: Spelling vs. Semantic Embeddings

Why Spelling Models Fail at Semantics

Character and n-gram models only look at surface character overlap. They treat "I love dogs" and "I adore puppies" as completely distinct because they share almost no common letters, despite meaning the exact same thing to a human.

How Modern LLMs Understand Meaning

Modern Transformer models (like BERT, GPT, and Gemini) map words into dense, high-dimensional vector spaces where synonyms, related concepts, and syntactic roles cluster together. Dimensionality reduction (like PCA or t-SNE) projects these into 3D for human exploration.
